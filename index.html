Curated Research Database on How AI Reshapes Thinking, Work & Self

Built for people who advise teams on AI adoption, write about it, teach it, or shape policy around it. Turn your generic AI into a research-grounded specialist.

Get the Knowledge Pack

AI is reshaping how we think, decide, and work. The research on what this means is scattered across disciplines, the field is new, and AI itself gives confident but shallow answers on this topic.

This knowledge base organizes 134 sources from cognitive science, HCI, organizational psychology, education research, and behavioral science into concepts that name what you're already experiencing, plus frameworks and practices you can apply. Give it to your AI, and it can help you write, teach, draft policy, or prepare an argument — grounded in research instead of generic knowledge.

What's Inside

229 entries: 73 concepts, 22 methods, and 134 sources. Every entry ties back to research or documented practice, status-tagged so you know the confidence level. Curated through an AI-assisted, human-reviewed process. Open source, MIT licensed. Current release: September 2026.
